About us

Compassion that started close to home.

Maryland Fur Babies grew from a simple act of kindness: feeding and protecting the stray cats and animals nobody else was looking after.

Who we are

Maryland Fur Babies Inc is a small, family run, grass roots animal rescue based in Baltimore, Maryland, with reach that extends well beyond the state. We are an IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) public charity (EIN 83-4101673) and have been incorporated since 2019.

It started with food. There were hungry cats outside and no one was feeding them, so we did. Then came the kittens dumped on a curb, the cat with an infected tail, the colony that nobody else was looking after. The food bills and vet bills grew, and we formed Maryland Fur Babies so the community could help share the cost of caring for them.

We have no paid staff and no office. The work is done by family, volunteers and fosters, which means donations go to the animals, not to overhead.

Our mission

Maryland Fur Babies is dedicated to rescuing, protecting and advocating for animals in need. We provide food, shelter, medical care and loving support to stray, abandoned and neglected animals while inspiring compassion and responsibility within our community.

Our goal is simple: give every fur baby the chance to live a safe, healthy and loved life.

What we do

  • Feed colonies and strays every day. We keep regular feeding sites for outdoor cat colonies and animals living on the street, marked with our own Maryland Fur Babies signs.
  • Get animals medical care. Flea, tick and deworming treatment, vaccines, treatment for injuries and infections, and emergency vet visits.
  • Spay and neuter. We help get community cats spayed and neutered so colonies stop growing and fewer kittens are born on the street.
  • Transport. We drive animals to the vet and back ourselves.
  • Find safe places. We work with fosters and fellow rescues to get abandoned kittens and other animals into safe homes.
  • Advocate. We share animals' stories online so more people learn about responsible pet care, spay/neuter and the animals that need help.

How we use donations

Donations pay for food and litter, flea and tick treatment, vaccines, spay/neuter surgeries, emergency vet visits and the gas to get animals to appointments. We do not pay salaries or rent. Donations are processed securely by PayPal and are tax-deductible as allowed by law.
