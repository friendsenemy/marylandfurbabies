Found an animal in need?

We are a small local rescue and are not able to take intake, adoption or placement requests online. If you have found an animal in need, please contact your county animal services or a shelter near you. See our pet resources page for what to do next. To support the animals already in our care, please donate here.

Privacy

This website does not collect or store payment-card or PayPal login information. Donations are processed by PayPal. If you email us, we use your address only to reply to you and never sell or share it. Our site uses a Google Ads tag to measure how many visitors reach our donation page.

Maryland Fur Babies

A small, family run, grass roots 501(c)(3) animal rescue based in Maryland with nationwide reach. We feed, treat and protect stray, abandoned and neglected animals.
