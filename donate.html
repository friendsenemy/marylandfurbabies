Questions about giving

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. Maryland Fur Babies Inc is an IRS-recognized 501(c)(3) public charity, EIN 83-4101673. Donations are tax-deductible as allowed by law. PayPal emails you a receipt for every gift.

Do I need a PayPal account?

No. On our PayPal donation page you can pay with any debit or credit card, your PayPal balance or Venmo. We never see or store your card details.

How do I make my gift monthly?

Click “Start a monthly donation,” then tick “Make this a monthly donation” on the PayPal page before you complete your gift. You can cancel any time from your PayPal account.

Where does the money go?

To the animals: food and litter, flea and tick treatment, vaccines, spay/neuter surgeries, emergency vet visits and transport to appointments. We have no paid staff and no office rent.

How can I check that you are a real charity?

You can verify our 501(c)(3) status on the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search using EIN 83-4101673, and see our Platinum Seal of Transparency on Candid (GuideStar).

Can I donate supplies instead?

Please contact us first so we can tell you what is needed right now.
