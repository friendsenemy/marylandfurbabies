How to help

Small actions can become second chances.

You do not need to live in Maryland to make a difference for these animals. Here is how you can help from anywhere.

Donate

Your gift pays for food, litter, medical care, spay/neuter and rides to the vet. Give once, or make it monthly so the food bowls stay full all year.

Share our mission

Follow and share Maryland Fur Babies on TikTok and Facebook. Every share puts these animals in front of someone new who might help.

Get your fur baby featured

Send us a photo or short video of your pet and it may be featured on our TikTok, Facebook and Instagram. Every feature helps us reach more people who can help.

Submit your fur baby →

Start a fundraiser for the animals

Birthdays, memorials and milestones are a great reason to ask friends to give. Facebook lets you start a fundraiser for a nonprofit in a few taps. Search for Maryland Fur Babies when you choose the charity, or start from our Facebook page.

Give monthly

Monthly donors are the steadiest help a small rescue can have. Food and litter are needed every single day, and vet emergencies do not wait for a good month. A recurring gift of any size lets us plan instead of scramble. You can start, change or cancel a monthly gift any time through PayPal.

Give through TikTok

Many of our supporters find us on TikTok. Tap the Donate button on any of our videos at @marylandfurbabies and your gift goes straight to the animals.

What your support pays for

  • Food and litter for the animals and colonies we feed every day
  • Flea, tick and deworming treatment
  • Vaccines and spay/neuter surgeries
  • Emergency vet visits and surgeries
  • Transport to and from the vet

Found an animal that needs help?

We are a small local rescue and are not able to take intake, adoption or placement requests online. If you have found an animal in need, please contact your county animal services or a shelter near you. Our pet resources page explains what to do.
