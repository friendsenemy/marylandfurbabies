What we do

Practical help. Compassionate care.

Rescue often begins with everyday necessities and someone willing to act. We work to connect vulnerable animals with the care and resources they need.

Food & shelter

Helping provide basic necessities and safer temporary environments for animals facing hardship.

Medical support

Supporting access to appropriate veterinary care, including spay and neuter needs when possible.

Rescue advocacy

Networking with caregivers, volunteers and community resources to find sensible paths forward.

About us

Compassion that started close to home.

Maryland Fur Babies grew from a simple act of kindness: Carole Ridgell, a Baltimore senior on a fixed income, feeding and protecting stray cats and other animals in her neighborhood. As the need grew, so did the mission.

✓Rescue and rehabilitate abandoned, stray and neglected animals.
✓Help connect animals with food, shelter and veterinary care.
✓Support spay/neuter awareness and responsible pet care.
✓Build stronger rescue partnerships and community involvement.

Our mission

Maryland Fur Babies is dedicated to rescuing, protecting and advocating for animals in need. We provide food, shelter, medical care and loving support to stray, abandoned and neglected animals while inspiring compassion and responsibility within our community.

Our goal is simple: give every fur baby the chance to live a safe, healthy and loved life.

Pet resources

When an animal needs help, the next call matters.

Emergency or injury

If an animal has life-threatening injuries or severe distress, contact an emergency veterinarian or appropriate local animal-control/public-safety resource immediately.

Stray or abandoned animal

Safely document the location and condition and contact local animal services or a rescue that serves the animal’s location and species.

Need help keeping a pet

Local shelters and pet-resource programs may offer food pantries, low-cost clinics or owner-support programs during temporary hardship.

Important: This website does not provide veterinary or emergency medical advice.
