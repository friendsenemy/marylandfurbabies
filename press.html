Boilerplate

Short (1 sentence): Maryland Fur Babies is a small, family run 501(c)(3) animal rescue based in Maryland that feeds, treats and protects stray and abandoned animals, funded entirely by donations.

Standard (1 paragraph): Maryland Fur Babies is a family run, grass roots 501(c)(3) animal rescue based in Maryland with nationwide reach. It began with feeding the stray cats nobody else was looking after. Today the organization provides food, litter, flea and tick treatment, vaccines, spay/neuter and emergency veterinary care for community cats, dumped kittens, dogs and other animals in need, working alongside fosters and fellow rescues. There are no salaries and no office: donations go to the animals. Donations are tax-deductible (EIN 83-4101673) and can be made at marylandfurbabies.org.

The story in brief

Maryland Fur Babies did not set out to be a nonprofit. It started with putting food out for hungry strays. Then came the kittens someone dumped on a curb, the cat with an infected tail, the colony nobody else was feeding. The food and vet bills grew, so the family formed Maryland Fur Babies in 2019 so the community could help share the cost.

In summer 2026 a post about four kittens abandoned on a street reached more than 6,000 people and was shared over 450 times; all four kittens were trapped and placed in foster homes within days. A photo of Tweety, a kitten thrown from a moving car who needed emergency care, was used by Show Your Soft Side to launch Project Kitty Angel, a trap-neuter-return initiative for the region's street cats.

Maryland Fur Babies focuses on the animals already in its care and in its immediate community; it does not take intake, adoption or placement requests online.

What donations pay for

Food and litter for colonies, flea, tick and deworming treatment, vaccines, spay/neuter surgeries, emergency vet visits and transport to appointments. Monthly gifts keep the food bowls full in quiet months.
